About the game

Survive as the wild. Protect it as a ranger.

A free 3D wildlife game set in five real Pakistani habitats. Survive as an animal, or protect them as a ranger. Many of these species are really endangered.

5

Real habitats

Thar Desert, Indus River Plains, Ziarat Juniper Forest, Deosai Plains and the Northern Mountains, with seasons and weather.

19

Species

From the Indus river dolphin to the snow leopard, each with its conservation status in your field guide.

3

Ways to play

Wild Life, Ranger Patrol and Photo Safari. You choose what to play every time.

Features

Built to be replayed

S

Real survival

Manage health, food, water, stamina and body temperature through seasons, sandstorms, rain and blizzards.

P

Poachers with a plan

Shikari patrol, set snares, and hunt animals with rifles. They also defend themselves, and cornered predators fight back.

Living animals

Herds flee, predators hunt, bears guard their ground. Animals have natural, hand-shaped bodies and walk cycles.

Rewards and ranks

Earn coins, XP and promotions from Trainee Ranger to Chief Warden. Chain spirit orbs for score combos.

F

Field guide

Photograph wildlife for star ratings. Rare animals score higher. Fill all 19 pages of your guide.

L

Leaderboards

Boards for high scores, species discovered, best photo and fastest rescue, plus your full profile record.

Wildlife

Meet the species

Every animal in the game is a real Pakistani species. Conservation status shown is from the IUCN Red List or national assessments.

Why it matters

Lessons from the wild

Poaching, snares and dams push species towards extinction. The markhor came back when villagers became its protectors.

~2,000Indus river dolphins left
<400snow leopards in Pakistan
1national animal brought back by community conservation: the markhor

Figures are approximate and change as surveys are updated. If you see wildlife crime, report it to your provincial wildlife department. Never buy skins, horns or wild pets.
